      How to Use FPV Builder

      Build your ideal FPV drone by selecting compatible parts step by step. The app checks compatibility as you go and shows live weight, price, and performance stats.
      🛸
      Pick Your Parts
      Click any component slot in the left panel — Frame, Motors, ESC, Flight Controller, Props, Camera, VTX, Battery, Receiver — to open the parts catalog on the right. Browse, search, sort by price or weight, and click a part to select it.
      🗺️
      Blueprint Diagram
      The center shows a live blueprint of your build. Parts light up blue when selected and amber when there's a compatibility warning. Click any part in the diagram to jump straight to its catalog.
      ⚡
      Quick Builds
      In a hurry? Scroll to the bottom of the left panel and tap a preset — 5" Freestyle, 5" Race, 3" Toothpick, LR 5", Micro 3.5" — to instantly fill all slots with a curated parts list. You can then swap individual parts to customise it.
      ⚖️
      Compare Parts
      Inside any catalog view, tap Compare, select 2–4 parts, then hit Compare (n) to see a side-by-side spec table. Great for deciding between motors or flight controllers.
      ⚠️
      Compatibility Warnings
      The app automatically checks motor-mount sizing, voltage ranges, ESC amp ratings, propeller shaft sizes, and stack dimensions. Warnings appear in the build card and highlight the offending part in amber on the diagram.
      💾
      Save & Share
      Save stores your build in the browser (no account needed). Builds lets you load or delete saved builds. Share copies a link you can send to anyone — the full build is encoded in the URL.
      📊
      Build Stats
      The build card shows total weight, estimated price (₱), thrust-to-weight ratio (TWR), and battery cell count — updating live as you select parts. A TWR above 4:1 is good for freestyle; above 6:1 for racing.
