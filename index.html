      How to Use FPV Builder

      Configure a custom FPV drone from 1058 real parts across 9 categories. The app checks compatibility as you build and shows live weight, price, and performance stats.
      🛸
      Pick Your Parts
      Click any slot in the left panel — Frame, Motors, ESC, Flight Controller, Props, Camera, VTX, Battery, Receiver — to open the parts catalog. Search by name, filter by brand or stock status, sort by price or weight, and click a card to select it. Hit Detail on any card for full specs and a retailer link.
      🗺️
      Blueprint Diagram
      The center panel shows a live top-down diagram of your build. Each component takes its part's actual color and displays the brand, name, and key spec. Parts glow blue when selected, turn amber on a compatibility warning, and pulse when active. Click any component to jump to its catalog.
      📷
      Parts Gallery
      Click Gallery in the diagram toolbar, or click the 📷 icon that appears on any selected component, to open a visual card grid of everything in your build — product photos, specs, prices, and retailer links all in one place.
      ⚡
      Preset Builds
      Scroll to the bottom of the left panel and pick a preset — 5" Freestyle, 5" Race, 3" Toothpick, Long-Range 5", Micro 3.5" — to instantly fill all 9 slots. Swap individual parts to customise from there.
      ⚖️
      Compare Parts
      Inside any catalog, tap Compare, select 2–4 parts, then hit Compare (n) to see a side-by-side spec table. Useful for choosing between motors or flight controllers.
      ⚠️
      Compatibility Warnings
      The app checks motor-mount sizing, voltage limits, ESC amp ratings, prop shaft sizes, stack dimensions, and digital FPV system pairing (DJI O3, Walksnail, HDZero). Violations appear in the build card and highlight the offending part amber on the diagram.
      💾
      Save & Share
      Save stores your build locally — no account needed. Builds lists all saved builds so you can load or delete them. Share encodes the full build into the URL — paste it anywhere and the recipient opens the exact same build.
      📊
      Build Stats
      Click Stats to see total weight, estimated price (₱), and thrust-to-weight ratio — all calculated live from your selected parts. A TWR above 4:1 is solid for freestyle; above 6:1 for racing.
